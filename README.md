# Priority Board

A one-page task organizer that runs in your web browser. Dump everything on your mind into a list, sort it onto a priority board, and run a focus timer on what you're working on.

There's nothing to install and no account to make.

---

## Getting started

1. **Open the file.** Double-click `Priority Board.html`. It opens in your default browser.
2. **Brain dump.** Type a task into the "Add to…" line in the **Brain Dump** panel and press **Enter**. You can paste several lines at once, and each line becomes its own task.
3. **Sort it.** Drag a task onto one of the colored boxes on the board. The task stays in your list and takes on that box's color, so you can see at a glance what's been sorted.
4. **Focus.** Drag a task onto the **Focus** timer, pick a time, and press **Start**.
5. **Check things off.** Tick the box next to a task when it's done.

Your work saves automatically as you go. Close the page and open it again, and everything is still there.

> **Tip:** Use **Settings → Export** now and then to save a backup file. See [Where your data lives](#where-your-data-lives).

## The layout

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Projects** (far left) | Longer-running work. Tasks you drag here move out of Brain Dump and live under the project. |
| **Brain Dump** | Your main list, split into categories like Work and Personal. |
| **Side buttons** | Clear Completed, Clear Board and Settings. On narrow windows there's also a Lists button. |
| **Board** | The colored priority boxes. |
| **Focus** (far right) | The timer, plus the tasks you're working on right now. |

### The default board

| Box | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Do Now** | Urgent and important |
| **Schedule** | Important, not urgent |
| **Timebox** | Give it a set slot today |
| **Quick Win** | Do it fast, keep it small |
| **Blocked** | Waiting on something |

You can rename every box and panel, change their colors, and split them. See [Customize](#customize).

## Everyday use

### Brain Dump
- **Add a task:** type in the "Add to…" line at the bottom of a category and press **Enter**. Pasted lines that start with `-`, `*` or `•` have the bullet removed.
- **Edit a task:** double-click its text, then press **Enter** to save or **Esc** to cancel.
- **Delete a task:** hover over it and click the **×**.
- **Add a category:** click **+ New category** at the bottom of the list.
- **Rename a category:** double-click its name.
- **Delete a category:** hover over its name and click the trash icon. If it has tasks, click a second time to confirm.

### Projects
- **Add a project:** click **+ New** and type a name.
- **Collapse or expand:** click the arrow next to the project name. A collapsed project still shows a done/total count, like "2/5", with a thin progress bar.
- **Fill it:** drag tasks from Brain Dump onto the project, or type them into its own "Add to…" line.
- **Delete a project:** hover over its name and click the trash icon. If it has tasks, click a second time to confirm. Its tasks go back to Brain Dump. Nothing is lost.

### The board
- **Sort a task:** drag it from your list onto a box.
- **Move a task between boxes:** drag the card from one box to another.
- **Take a task off the board:** click the **×** on its card, or drag the card back onto the list. The task stays in your list. It just loses its color.
- **Find a task in your list:** click its card on the board. The list scrolls to it and briefly highlights it.
- Each box shows how many tasks it holds, like "Timebox 2 items".

### Side buttons
- **Clear Completed** takes every checked-off task off the board in one click. The tasks stay in your list, still checked. The button is greyed out when there's nothing to clear.
- **Clear Board** takes *every* task off the board, with a confirmation pop-up first. Your tasks, notes and check-offs all stay.
- **Settings** opens Customize, Timer, Export and Import.
- **Lists** appears only on narrower windows (see [Narrow windows](#narrow-windows)).

### Focus timer
- **Start / Pause / Resume** and **Reset** control the countdown.
- **Set the length** with the **−** and **+** buttons, by typing a number (1 to 180 minutes), or with one of the four quick-timer buttons. You can only change the length while the timer is stopped.
- **Add what you're working on** by dragging any task onto the timer panel. It shows under **Working on** and stays where it was everywhere else. Click the **×** to take it off the timer.
- While the timer runs, the browser tab shows the countdown and your first task, so you can keep an eye on it from another tab.
- When time's up, a soft chime plays **once**. There's no repeating alarm.

## The details

### Notes
Every task can have a note for details, links or next steps.

- **Add or edit a note:** hover over a task and click the **notepad icon**. It's also on cards on the board.
- **Save:** click **Save** or press **Ctrl+Enter**. Clicking anywhere outside the note also saves.
- **Cancel:** click **Cancel** or press **Esc**.
- **Read a note:** hover over a task that has one, and the note pops up after a moment. Tasks with notes always show the notepad icon.
- **Remove a note:** open it and click **Delete note**, or clear the text and save.
- Notes stay attached when a task moves between categories, projects and boxes.

### How drag and drop works
Pick up a task by clicking and holding anywhere on it except the checkbox or buttons, then move it. Drop zones show a dashed outline when you're over them. On a touchscreen, drag by the **dotted grip** on the left of a list task.

| You drag… | …onto | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| A list task | A board box | The task gets that box's color. It **stays** in the list. |
| A list task | The Focus timer | The task is added under "Working on". It **stays** where it was. |
| A list task | A project | The task **moves** into the project and out of Brain Dump. |
| A list task | Another Brain Dump category | The task **moves** to that category. |
| A board card | Another box | The task changes boxes. |
| A board card | The list area | The task comes off the board and stays in the list. |
| A board card | The Focus timer | The task is added under "Working on". |

A drag only starts once you've moved a few pixels, so a plain click on a card still finds it in the list instead of dragging it.

### Narrow windows
When the window is narrow, Projects and Brain Dump tuck into a slide-out panel so the board and timer have the room.

- Click **Lists** to slide the panel out. Click the dimmed area, press **Esc**, or click Lists again to close it.
- Dragging still works from the panel. Once you drag past the panel's edge it slides away, so you can drop onto the board or the timer.

### Settings
Click **Settings** on the side to open the menu.

#### Customize
- **Theme:** Match computer (the default), Light, Dark or Sepia. Clicking one previews it right away, and Save keeps it.
- **Panels:** rename "Projects" and "Brain Dump" to anything you like. The new names are used everywhere on the page.
- **Board:** each quadrant (top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right) has its own card.
  - **Color, Title, Hint:** pick from 10 colors and set the name and the short hint text. The hint can be left blank.
  - **Split in two:** adds a second box under the first one. The board always has between **4 and 8** boxes.
  - **Merge into one:** keeps the top box. Tasks in the bottom box move into it.
- Nothing changes until you click **Save**. **Cancel** throws away everything you tried. **Reset all to defaults** puts back the original theme, names and five boxes.
- **Shortcut:** double-click any box title, box hint or panel name on the page to rename it in place.

#### Timer
- **Quick timers:** set the four quick-timer buttons, 1 to 180 minutes each. The defaults are 15, 25, 30 and 60.
- **Chime:** Soft ding (the default), Single bell, Rising three notes, Marimba, Glass tap, or No sound. Click **Preview** to hear one before saving.

#### Export and Import
- **Export** downloads a backup file named like `priority-board-backup-2026-11-09.json`. It holds everything: tasks, categories, projects, notes, the board layout, names, colors, theme and timer settings.
- **Import** restores from one of those files. It **replaces** what's on the page now, so export first if you want to keep the current state.

### Where your data lives
- Everything is saved **inside the browser** that opens the file, on that computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Each browser keeps its own copy. Chrome and Edge won't see each other's tasks, so stick to one browser.
- Clearing your browser's site data or cookies can erase the board. **Export regularly** so you always have a backup.
- To move to another computer or browser, Export on the old one and Import on the new one.
- If the board is open in two tabs, they stay in sync.
- The timer keeps counting if you close the page. Reopen it early and it picks up where it should be. If it ran out while the page was closed, it shows "Time's up" without playing a sound.

### Keyboard shortcuts
| Key | Where | Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Enter** | "Add to…" line | Adds the task |
| **Enter** | While renaming | Saves the new name |
| **Esc** | While renaming, in a note, in a dialog, in a menu | Cancels or closes |
| **Ctrl+Enter** | In a note | Saves the note |
| **↑ / ↓** | Settings menu | Moves between options |
